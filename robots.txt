# Songwunsch -- what search engines may index.
#
# The start page (the repertoire) and the pages -- imprint, privacy notice,
# FAQ -- are public information. Everything that carries names stays out:
# the rooms (a room is often named after the hosts of a private event), the
# wish lists and the suggestions (they show the names guests gave), the name
# form and the operating pages behind the sign-in. This keeps well-behaved
# crawlers away; it is not access control.
#
# Works only with the application at the domain root, since a robots.txt is
# always read from /robots.txt. Under a sub-path, merge these lines into the
# domain's own robots.txt with the sub-path in front of every address.

User-agent: *
Disallow: /rooms
Disallow: /wishes
Disallow: /suggestions
Disallow: /name
Disallow: /login
Disallow: /settings
Disallow: /users
Disallow: /admin
Disallow: /song
